Needle & Sign

Every speed sign in Ghana is in km/h. A lot of the cars on our roads came from the US or UK, and their speedometers show mph first. Pick a vehicle and a real route and see what the limits actually mean on your dial. Cameras flash, fines add up, and six flashes costs you your licence.

Cheat sheet

The limits, in both units

Prototype values: 30 near schools, 50 in towns, 80 on regional roads, 90 on national highways and 100 on motorways for light vehicles. Heavy vehicles (over 3.5 t) get 70 on the open road and 80 on the motorway. Check these against the gazetted L.I. 2519 schedule before you use this publicly. Posted signs always win.

Reading an mph dial

Where your needle should sit

On a US or UK car the big numbers are mph and km/h is the small inner ring, if the car has one at all. 1 mph = 1.609 km/h. A quick rule: take 60% of the sign and you have mph.
