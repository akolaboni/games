Reading an mph dial

Where your needle should sit

On US and UK cars the big numbers are mph. km/h is the small inner ring, if there is one. Quick rule: take 60% of the sign.

When a real notice arrives

How Traffitech-GH works

  • Fixed cameras, mobile units and laser speed guns send an Electronic Notice of Violation (ENV) by SMS to the registered owner, whoever was driving.
  • Pay or contest within 14 days. After that, 1% of the fine is added for every day late.
  • Unpaid fines block DVLA services: no licence renewal, transfer or roadworthy inspection.
  • Nobody has to warn you about cameras. Some stretches have warning signs, some don't.

Don't get scammed

Real notices never have links

  • The sender ID must be GPS-MTTD. Anything else, or any message with a clickable link, is likely a scam.
  • Pay only through ghana.gov.gh, or dial *222# and choose option 6.
  • Check a car for outstanding fines with *220#, before you buy one too.
  • Never pay an officer in cash or to a personal MoMo number. Report it on 191 or 18555.

The notices in this game are practice cards. They are not real ENVs.
