When a real notice arrives

  • It comes by SMS to the registered owner, whoever was driving.
  • Pay or contest within 14 days. After that, 1% more every day.
  • Unpaid fines block DVLA renewals and transfers.
  • Real notices come from sender GPS-MTTD and never contain links. Pay only at ghana.gov.gh or *222# option 6. Check a car with *220#.

The notices in this game are practice cards, not real ones.
