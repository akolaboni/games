The rules

How you score

  • Pace: hold the needle between 85% and 100% of the limit. The longer you hold it, the higher the multiplier, up to ×5.
  • Perfect pass: go past a camera inside the band for a bonus.
  • Clean stop: stop before the line at a red light.
  • Heat: every notice adds a bar. At 3 bars, or if a laser gun catches you, an MTTD patrol pulls in front. Stop, or you're busted. Six notices and your licence is suspended.

From the Ghana Police Traffitech-GH FAQ on L.I. 2519. They apply whether or not a sign is posted. Trucks and buses: 60 on highways, 80 on motorways unverified (MTTD figures; the L.I. 2519 vehicle table isn't published online yet).

What gets you a notice

  • Speeding, caught by a fixed camera, a mobile unit or a laser gun.
  • Red light: if your tyres cross the stop line after it turns red. Amber is not a red-light offence.
  • Driving without your seatbelt.
  • Wrongful overtaking, such as crossing a solid line.
  • Each offence is a separate notice, even at the same junction.

When a real one arrives

  • It comes by SMS to the registered owner, whoever was driving. Pay or contest within 14 days, then 1% more every day.
  • Real notices come from sender GPS-MTTD and never contain links. Pay only at ghana.gov.gh or *222# option 6. Check any car with *220#.
  • At a roadside stop, officers use a handheld PDA. You don't have to carry your licence, but know its number.

The notices in this game are practice cards, not real ones.
