Limits from the Ghana Police Traffitech-GH FAQ on L.I. 2519. They apply whether or not a sign is posted. Stopping distances are typical for a car on a dry road (thinking plus braking). Trucks and buses: 60 on highways, 80 on motorways unverified; the L.I. 2519 vehicle table isn't published online yet.

Why 30 near schools

At 30 km/h a car stops in about 11 m. At 50 it needs about 24 m, more than double. A child stepping out 14 m ahead is safe at 30, and not at 40.

What gets you a notice

  • Speeding, caught by a fixed camera, a mobile unit or a laser gun. Cameras don't have to be signposted.
  • Red light: if your tyres cross the stop line after it turns red. Amber is not a red-light offence.
  • Driving without your seatbelt.
  • Wrongful overtaking, such as crossing a solid line.
  • Each offence is its own notice, even at the same junction.

When a real notice arrives

  • It comes by SMS to the registered owner, whoever was driving. Pay or contest within 14 days, then 1% more every day.
  • Unpaid fines block DVLA renewals, transfers and roadworthy checks.
  • Real notices come from sender GPS-MTTD and never contain links. Pay only at ghana.gov.gh or *222# option 6. Check any car with *220#.

The notices in this game are practice cards, not real ones.
