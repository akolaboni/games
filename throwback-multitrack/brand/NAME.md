# Crate Five — working name

**Why:** Players pull one song from a crate of three sleeves while five instrument drops count down their possible points. “Crate” feels physical and DJ-led; “Five” gives the score and logo a shape.

**Pronunciation:** Crate Five. Visual shorthand: `CRATE/5` or the tilted numeral mark.

**Promise:** Name it before the full mix.

**Other directions considered:** Drop Five (more literal, less tied to the rack); First Drop (makes the early-guess goal clear but feels broad); Needle Drop (strong music phrase, already used by other music games). Crate Five is a working design direction, not a trademark or domain clearance.

**Logo behavior:** The 5 sits in a slightly rotated coral sleeve, with five short bars below CRATE. At tiny sizes, use the icon: one record, the 5, five stem bars. Keep the mark on a solid surface, not over a photo.

**Mood:** 1980s hi-fi rack meets a hand-stamped record sleeve. Dark ink, varnished wood edges, warm yellow display, coral pick cards, mint score light. Surfaces have depth; controls remain crisp and readable. Motion comes from the sound: record rotation, channel fader drop, sleeve deal, result-card slap.

**Next creative decision:** Confirm or reject “Crate Five” before changing the public slug (`/throwback-multitrack/`). The current UI and result text use it as a working title.
